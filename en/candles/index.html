🕯️ Scented Candles & Wax Melts

Natural wax, handmade in Bulgaria — a touch of magic in the air

Coziness you can feel

Our scented candles and wax melts are handmade from natural stearin wax — phthalate-free, decorated with beads and natural stones. Drop one melt into an aroma burner — and the room fills with mood. Perfect as a gift, even better for yourself. And if you dream of something special — we also make custom shaped candles to order.
